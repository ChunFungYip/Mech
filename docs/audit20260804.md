建議的審核修正已在目前工作區完成。專案現在可以通過 Godot 4.7.1 編輯器載入，以及無頭模式主場景執行冒煙測試。

## Resolved Findings / 已修正問題

### 1. Invalid 3D fade property / 無效的 3D 淡出屬性

**Finding:** Generated sparks and smoke puffs attempted to tween `modulate:a`, which is a 2D `CanvasItem` property and is not valid for `MeshInstance3D`.

**Fix:** Effects now enable alpha transparency on their `StandardMaterial3D` and animate the material alpha with a tween method. The effect node is then queued for deletion.

Reference: [scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd)

**問題：** 生成的火花和煙霧曾經使用 `modulate:a`，但這是 2D `CanvasItem` 屬性，不適用於 `MeshInstance3D`。

**修正：** 效果現在會啟用 `StandardMaterial3D` 的透明度，使用 tween 方法修改材質 alpha，完成後以 `queue_free()` 清理節點。

參考：[scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd)

### 2. Boss state consistency / Boss 狀態一致性

**Finding:** Leaving the boss area cleared the area flag even after the boss had been activated. The objective and boss HP panel could therefore contradict the real encounter state.

**Fix:** Added persistent `_boss_engaged` state. Once activated, the objective remains focused on defeating the boss and the boss HP panel remains available until the boss is defeated.

Reference: [scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd), [scripts/GameHUD.gd](../scripts/GameHUD.gd)

**問題：** 玩家離開 Boss 區域後，即使 Boss 已經啟動，區域狀態仍會被清除，導致任務目標和 Boss 生命值介面與實際戰鬥狀態不一致。

**修正：** 新增持久的 `_boss_engaged` 狀態。Boss 一旦啟動，任務目標會持續顯示擊敗 Boss，Boss HP 介面會維持到 Boss 被摧毀為止。

參考：[scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd)、[scripts/GameHUD.gd](../scripts/GameHUD.gd)

### 3. Boss hangar safety / Boss 與機庫安全距離

**Finding:** Regular enemies were kept out of the hangar, but the Boss was excluded from that protection and could chase the player too close to the home base.

**Fix:** The Boss now uses the protected chase position, is included in the hangar repulsion loop, and has its own `keep_out_of_hangar()` implementation.

Reference: [scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd), [scripts/BossMech.gd](../scripts/BossMech.gd)

**問題：** 普通敵人會被阻擋在機庫外，但 Boss 被排除在保護之外，可能追擊玩家到基地附近。

**修正：** Boss 現在使用受保護的追擊位置，加入機庫排斥檢查，並在 `BossMech.gd` 實作自己的 `keep_out_of_hangar()`。

參考：[scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd)、[scripts/BossMech.gd](../scripts/BossMech.gd)

### 4. Configurable missile salvo size / 可調整的導彈齊射數量

**Finding:** `missile_salvo_size` was exported but the actual launch count, formation, and UI strings were hardcoded to eight.

**Fix:** The salvo size is passed from `MechPlayer.gd` to `Main.gd`, the launch offsets are generated from the configured count, and player status/announcement text uses the configured value.

Reference: [scripts/MechPlayer.gd](../scripts/MechPlayer.gd), [scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd)

**問題：** `missile_salvo_size` 雖然是可匯出的參數，但實際發射數量、編隊位置和 UI 文字都固定為八枚。

**修正：** 齊射數量由 `MechPlayer.gd` 傳遞到 `Main.gd`，發射位置會依設定數量動態生成，玩家狀態和提示文字也會使用實際設定值。

參考：[scripts/MechPlayer.gd](../scripts/MechPlayer.gd)、[scripts/Main.gd](../scripts/Main.gd)

### 5. Actor-relative firing origins / 依照機體方向的武器發射點

**Finding:** Enemy and Boss firing origins used world-space negative Z offsets, which could place tracers on the wrong side after rotation.

**Fix:** Firing origins now use `global_transform.basis` so local muzzle offsets follow actor rotation.

Reference: [scripts/EnemyMech.gd](../scripts/EnemyMech.gd), [scripts/BossMech.gd](../scripts/BossMech.gd)

**問題：** 敵人和 Boss 的武器發射點使用世界座標負 Z 偏移，機體旋轉後可能從錯誤的一側產生曳光彈。

**修正：** 發射點現在使用 `global_transform.basis`，本地武器偏移會跟隨機體旋轉。

參考：[scripts/EnemyMech.gd](../scripts/EnemyMech.gd)、[scripts/BossMech.gd](../scripts/BossMech.gd)

### 6. Repair menu refresh cost / 維修選單更新效能

**Finding:** Every credit/profile signal could rebuild the upgrade controls while the repair menu was hidden.

**Fix:** Upgrade rows now rebuild only when the repair menu is open and the upgrade page is active. Deferred refresh and `queue_free()` remain in place for safe signal handling.

Reference: [scripts/RepairBotMenu.gd](../scripts/RepairBotMenu.gd)

**問題：** 每次獲得 credits 或 profile 更新時，即使維修選單隱藏，仍可能重新建立升級控制項。

**修正：** 只有在維修選單開啟且目前位於升級頁面時才重建升級列；同時保留 deferred refresh 和 `queue_free()`，避免信號發送期間釋放按鈕。

參考：[scripts/RepairBotMenu.gd](../scripts/RepairBotMenu.gd)

### 7. Procedural audio noise cost / 程序化音效雜訊成本

**Finding:** Random noise was generated for every audio sample in the main-thread mixer.

**Fix:** A 512-entry noise table is generated once at audio startup and reused during mixing. Headless mode skips audio initialization for clean automated shutdown.

Reference: [scripts/AudioManager.gd](../scripts/AudioManager.gd)

**問題：** 主執行緒音效混音器曾經為每個音訊 sample 產生隨機雜訊。

**修正：** 音效啟動時只建立 512 個數值的雜訊表，混音時重複取用；無頭模式會跳過音效初始化，確保自動測試乾淨結束。

參考：[scripts/AudioManager.gd](../scripts/AudioManager.gd)

### 8. Respawn invulnerability / 重生無敵時間

**Finding:** Player reset immediately returned the mech to the home base with no protection from continuing enemy fire.

**Fix:** Added configurable `respawn_invulnerability_seconds`, currently `3 seconds`. Sprint heat is also reset when the cockpit resets.

Reference: [scripts/MechPlayer.gd](../scripts/MechPlayer.gd)

**問題：** 玩家重置時會立即回到基地，但沒有短暫保護，可能再次被持續的敵火擊中。

**修正：** 新增可調整的 `respawn_invulnerability_seconds`，目前設定為 `3 秒`。駕駛艙重置時也會清除 Sprint 熱量狀態。

參考：[scripts/MechPlayer.gd](../scripts/MechPlayer.gd)

## Remaining Test Gaps / 尚未覆蓋的測試

The runtime smoke test confirms startup and shutdown, but it does not simulate a complete combat session. These scenarios should still be tested interactively:

- Fire rockets and missiles and confirm the smoke/debris fade and cleanup.
- Activate the Boss, leave the area, and verify the objective and boss bar remain coherent.
- Retreat to the hangar after Boss activation and verify the Boss stops outside the safety radius.
- Change `missile_salvo_size` and verify the actual number of missiles and UI text.
- Rotate enemies and Bosses before firing and inspect projectile origins.
- Earn credits with the repair menu closed and confirm no upgrade-list churn.
- Stress-test many simultaneous procedural audio voices.
- Verify the three-second respawn invulnerability during sustained enemy fire.
- Trigger player defeat, restart from both checkpoints, and confirm the overlay pauses and resumes the scene correctly.
- Let the boss reach phases 2 and 3 and confirm missile salvos, hazard warnings, damage ticks, and cleanup.
- Use the EMP pulse near each enemy archetype and confirm damage, stagger, cooldown, and HUD status.
- Save and load each campaign slot, including a completed mission, then restart the project and confirm the selected slot persists.
- Save during an active wave, reload, and verify player loadout, enemy component health, wave number, checkpoint, and boss phase are restored.
- Open the tutorial and settings pages at the minimum viewport and verify the controls page scrolls without clipping.

目前的冒煙測試只確認啟動和關閉，尚未模擬完整戰鬥流程。以下情境仍應在 Godot 中互動測試：

- 發射火箭和導彈，確認煙霧與碎片會淡出並清理。
- 啟動 Boss 後離開區域，確認任務目標和 Boss HP 條保持一致。
- Boss 啟動後退回機庫，確認 Boss 會停留在安全距離外。
- 修改 `missile_salvo_size`，確認實際導彈數量和 UI 文字同步。
- 先讓敵人和 Boss 旋轉再開火，檢查發射點位置。
- 在維修選單關閉時取得 credits，確認不會重建升級列表。
- 壓力測試大量同時存在的程序化音效。
- 在持續敵火下確認三秒重生無敵時間。
- 觸發玩家失敗，從兩個檢查點重啟，確認介面會暫停並正確恢復場景。
- 讓 Boss 進入第二及第三階段，確認導彈齊射、危險區域警示、傷害週期及清理流程。
- 在各種敵人附近使用 EMP，確認傷害、硬直、冷卻時間及 HUD 狀態。
- 儲存及載入每個戰役欄位，包括已完成任務，重啟專案後確認選定欄位仍然保留。
- 在戰鬥波次中儲存再載入，確認玩家裝備、敵人部件生命值、波次編號、檢查點及 Boss 階段都會恢復。
- 在最小視窗開啟教學及設定頁面，確認控制頁可捲動且沒有內容裁切。
